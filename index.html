🤖 Long stream? Let a free chatbot pick the moments NEW

A chatbot given a 9-hour transcript only really reads the start. So: split the transcript into small parts, paste each part into any free chatbot (Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude…), paste its answers back here, and NeonClipping downloads only those sections and makes the clips. Needs a YouTube video with captions. Nothing is sent anywhere by NeonClipping.

👥 Creators NEW

add a streamer once, clips start by themselves when they go live
NeonClipping checks every minute. When a creator on your list goes live, it records the stream and clips the chat spikes with the layout, facecam boxes and caption style you had selected when you added them (press “Use my current layout” to change it later). One stream at a time. Keep NeonClipping open; unattended recordings stop after 3 hours (a new one starts if they are still live), and the big recording file is deleted after the clips are cut.

⚡ Live clipping NEW

clips while the stream is on
Records the stream, watches chat speed and cuts a clip around every chat spike (starting ~24 s before it). Uses your layout, facecam boxes, caption style and language from the steps below. Works only while the stream is live.
